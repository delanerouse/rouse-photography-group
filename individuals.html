The Signature Session.

Most sessions run one to two hours. We'll spend as much time as it takes to get frames you're proud of.

How we work

We shoot tethered to a large monitor, so you see every frame as it's made. You're not guessing at what the camera caught. We adjust posture, expression, and light together until it's right.

You're in control. You decide how many outfits to bring, how long we keep going, and which images get finished. Your session fee covers the time. You pay for the images you choose, and nothing else.

Selection happens the same day, in the studio, on the big screen. You leave knowing exactly what you're getting.

What you get

  • A fully coached session, including facial expression coaching. Nobody is born knowing how to look natural in front of a camera. That's our job.
  • Unlimited time and outfits, within reason. Bring three looks or one. We don't clock-watch.
  • Same-day selection on a large monitor with your photographer.
  • Professional retouching included on every image you choose. Stray hairs, blemishes, teeth, softened lines. You still look like you.
  • High-resolution files, sized for print and web, delivered fast.

Good questions.

What should I wear?

Solid colors over patterns, and something you feel like yourself in. Bring two or three options and we'll pick together. For most professional headshots, a jacket or structured layer photographs well. Avoid logos and very thin stripes.

How long does it take?

Plan for one to two hours including selection. If you're on a tight schedule, tell us when you arrive and we'll work efficiently. If you want to keep going, we keep going.

How many images do most people choose?

Two to four is typical: a primary headshot, a second expression or crop, and sometimes a different outfit for a different use. There's no minimum.

How does hair and makeup work?

A professional artist meets you at the studio before your session. It adds about 45 minutes and $300. It's optional, and it makes a visible difference on camera for most people, men included.

When will I get my files?

Retouched, high-resolution files are delivered by download link, typically within a few business days of your session. Need them faster for a deadline? Tell us when you book.

Can my firm pay for this?

Often, yes. Many firms reimburse professional headshots or already have a corporate account with us. If your organization needs portraits for several people, our corporate programs are usually the better fit.
