For office administrators.

Most corporate accounts are managed by one coordinator who books for the whole team. Here is what you need to know.

Booking for someone else

Enter the colleague's name and email at checkout so the confirmation and reminders go to them. You can add your own email as a copy so you stay in the loop.

Booking several people at once

Book each person as a separate appointment in consecutive time slots. For groups of eight or more, or for a full-day on-site session, call us and we will block the day for you.

Rescheduling or cancelling

Use the link in the confirmation email to move or cancel a session. Once a session is booked, that time is reserved exclusively for your team, so please review our cancellation and rescheduling policy. If something comes up last minute, call us and we will work with you.

Session packages and codes

If your firm has a prepaid session package, enter your code at checkout. You can check the remaining balance on a code any time from the scheduler.

Your account rate and invoicing

Each corporate account has its own terms. Your rate, billing contact, and delivery timeline were confirmed when your account was set up. Questions about any of it, call 202.257.3918.

Where sessions take place

On-site at your office, or at our studio at 4501 Ford Avenue, Suite 107, Alexandria, Virginia 22302. Many firms prefer on-site so the team stays productive; the studio is always available as the simpler option.
